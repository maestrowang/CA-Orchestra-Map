Sources. High school courses come from the UC A-G Course List for 2026-27 (Visual & Performing Arts, Music), searched by title. Middle schools and a cross-check for public high schools come from the California Department of Education course enrollment file for 2018-19, course code 2303 Orchestra/symphony. Private middle schools are not covered.

Read with care. The CDE data is several years old, so some programs have opened or closed since. Each circle sits at the average location of that city's public schools, so it is accurate to the city and not to a school address. Full detail, including ambiguous course titles, is in the Orchestra Programs tab of the source spreadsheet.
